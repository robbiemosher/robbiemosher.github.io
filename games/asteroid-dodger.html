Asteroid Dodger

A tiny arcade game. Clear each level by destroying every asteroid, survive faster waves, and fight hostile ships in the final levels.

Rotate: A/D or ←/→  ·  Thrust: W/S or ↑/↓  ·  Shoot: Space  ·  Ability: E

You begin with three lives and gain one life after every cleared level. Fast clears earn more upgrade points. Between levels you can buy shields or a two-second spinning laser. Press E to use the spinning laser once purchased.
