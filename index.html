Hello Friends, Family and the Odd Stranger

I'm Robbie Mosher, a graduate student studying physics at McGill University in Montreal, Canada.

This is mostly a place for me to keep track of what I'm doing, what I'm learning, and whatever ideas happen to occupy my attention long enough to be written down. That will usually mean physics, mathematics, research and philosophy, but I make no promises about staying on topic.

Part of the reason for keeping this site is simply to have a record. Academic life moves quickly, and I like the idea of being able to look back later and see what I was thinking about, what I was working on, and how much of it turned out to be wrong.

Currently

I'm finishing my time in Halifax before moving to Montreal to begin graduate school. At the same time, I'm winding down a nearly five-year career as a bartender, packing up my life, and dealing with the many small administrative annoyances that apparently accompany moving between provinces.

The quieter parts of my days are increasingly spent reading about my new area of research: materials modelling, particularly phase-field crystal models and the physics of crystalline materials. At the moment, that means a great deal of reading, note-taking, and periodically realizing that I have misunderstood something several pages earlier.
